Privacy Policy

Chronicles of Elderon · last updated 2 November 2026

Chronicles of Elderon is a single-player game. This policy exists because Google Play asks every app to have one, and it is short because there is almost nothing to say.

What the game collects

The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

The Android app from the project's GitHub releases declares no permissions at all, including the permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

The Google Play edition is different in one way. It opens the game's website in Chrome, so the game's files are fetched from that site, and it sells the full game and the expansion through Google Play. After a purchase, the game sends the purchase's details from Google Play (the app's package name, which item was bought and Google Play's purchase token) over an encrypted connection to a small service of ours on Google Cloud. That service asks Google Play to confirm and acknowledge the purchase, which Google Play requires, and keeps nothing afterwards. Until that check succeeds, the token waits in the game's own storage on your device. The game never sees your card or Google account details; Google Play handles the payment.

What the game stores

Your saved games, up to three, and your settings: the sound and music levels, the battle speed, the language, which page of the camp you were on, and whether the first chapter's guide has been shown. They are kept in the app's private storage on your own device, and only there. The Android app opts out of Android's automatic backup entirely, so a saved game never leaves the device this way either, including when the device itself is backed up or replaced.

A save code is the one way a game leaves the device, and only by your hand: Load Game → Export turns a slot into a block of text that you can copy, share or save as a file yourself, and Import a save code reads one in. The game never sends a code anywhere.

Uninstalling the app deletes the saved games. Inside the game, Delete in Load Game deletes one slot.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, children included. The purchase details above are the only thing that leaves the device, and only when a purchase is made.

Third parties

No third-party code runs inside the game. In the Google Play edition, Google Play handles payments under Google's own privacy policy, the game's website is served by GitHub Pages, and the purchase check runs on Google Cloud.

Changes

If any of the above ever changes, this document will change first, and the date at the top with it.
