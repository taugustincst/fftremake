Chronicles of Elderon — Play Billing simulation harness

Elderon is a paid download: every chapter of the first game is included, and nothing locks one in any build. The one thing sold inside the Play app is The Hollow Sky expansion (product hollow_sky), through a native Play Billing bridge (window.ElderonPlay) that answers by calling window.__elderonPlayReply(json).

This page loads the real game below and switches on the simulation built into js/billing.js: a stand-in for the bridge that answers through the same reply channel. Everything after that boundary (the gate, the remembered ownership, the purchase outcomes) is the game's real code — the same code node tools/test-billing.js checks.

Run it: from the repository root, python3 -m http.server 8125, then open http://localhost:8125/tools/store-sim.html.

On the title screen press The Hollow Sky. Where the store gates it and it is not owned, the unlock screen appears with Buy and Restore; otherwise the campaign begins. With “offline” and the box above ticked, the remembered purchase keeps it open; with “not bought” and the box ticked, Play's answer (not owned) closes it again, as a refund would.

What this cannot prove (only a real Play Console listing can): that Google's servers accept the purchase, that the purchase sheet renders, and that the native side acknowledges purchases. The simulation never engages inside the Play app (the real bridge always wins) and answers only to this page's flags.
