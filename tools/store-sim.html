Chronicles of Elderon — Play Store simulation harness

This page proves the demo gate and the purchase flow without Google Play, which does not exist for this app yet (no Play Console account, no product). It loads the real game below and switches on the simulation built into js/billing.js: a stand-in for the one boundary only Google can provide — the Digital Goods service and the payment sheet. Everything on this side of that boundary (detection, gate arming, persistence, purchase bookkeeping) is the game's real code, the same code the Node suite checks: node tools/test-billing.js.

Run it: from the repository root, python3 -m http.server 8125, then open http://localhost:8125/tools/store-sim.html.

1. Pick a store scenario

2. Jump to the demo boundary

Then, in the game below, press March to Battle: with “not bought” the unlock screen appears instead of the chapter; buying there opens the road, and with “already bought” the chapter begins at once. Camp → Options shows the unlock card while the game is unbought. In the “pending” scenario the purchase waits on Play; switch the scenario to “already bought” and use Restore purchase on the unlock screen to watch it land.

What this cannot prove (only a real Play Console listing can): that Google's servers accept the purchase, that the payment sheet renders, and the acknowledgement step — Play auto-refunds a purchase that is not acknowledged through the Play Developer API within three days, which needs a small backend and is listed as a launch step in android/PLAY_STORE.md. The simulation also never engages in the shipped game by itself: it answers only to this page's flags, and on the open web the gate cannot arm in any case.
